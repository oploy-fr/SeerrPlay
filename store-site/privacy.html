Privacy Support

Effective July 29, 2026

Privacy policy

SeerrPlay is designed to minimize data collection and keep users in control.

Direct server communication

Searches, requests, media information, credentials and playback state are exchanged directly with the Seerr and personal media servers selected by the user. SeerrPlay does not operate an intermediary cloud service.

Data stored on the device

Profiles and preferences are stored locally. Authentication secrets use secure storage provided by the operating system. Offline media is stored locally until the user deletes it.

Google Cast

On supported Android devices, the Google Cast SDK may process technical application, device discovery and cast-session information. Media-server credentials are not intentionally sent to Google by SeerrPlay.

Tracking and advertising

SeerrPlay does not currently include advertising, developer analytics or cross-application tracking.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a local profile removes its connection details and credentials from the device. Offline downloads can be removed from the Downloads page. SeerrPlay cannot delete an account managed by an independent server administrator.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when SeerrPlay data practices change. Questions can be submitted through the support page.
